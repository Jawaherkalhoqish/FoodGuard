# 🌴 FoodGuard AI

## Early Stress Monitoring for Date Palms

FoodGuard AI is an AI-based decision-support prototype designed to help detect early signs of stress in date palms before visible symptoms become severe.

The project combines plant electrical signals with environmental and irrigation data to provide an early, explainable indication of palm stress, helping users understand the level of risk and decide what action should be taken next.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

Date palms can experience stress due to changes in soil conditions, temperature, weather, irrigation, and other environmental factors.

In many cases, visible symptoms may appear only after the stress has already developed.

FoodGuard AI explores a more proactive approach:

> Detect stress early, understand the risk, and take action sooner.

The system brings together multiple signals and data sources to support early monitoring and informed decision-making.

---

## 💡 The Problem

Traditional monitoring can depend heavily on visual inspection and manual observation.

However, by the time visible symptoms appear, the palm may already be experiencing significant stress.

This creates a need for a monitoring approach that can:

- Detect potential stress earlier
- Combine multiple environmental indicators
- Make complex data easier to understand
- Provide clear risk levels
- Support faster decision-making

---

## 🌱 The Solution

FoodGuard AI combines different sources of information to create a more complete view of palm condition.

### Data Sources

- 🌿 Plant electrical signals
- 🌱 Soil conditions
- 🌡️ Temperature
- ☁️ Weather conditions
- 💧 Irrigation data

Instead of relying on a single measurement, FoodGuard looks at the relationship between multiple signals to identify possible stress patterns.

---

## ⚙️ How FoodGuard Works

Plant & Environmental Data
↓
Signal Collection
↓
AI Analysis
↓
Stress Assessment
↓
Risk Level
↓
Recommended Next Action

### 01 — Sense

Collect relevant signals from the date palm and its surrounding environment.

### 02 — Analyze

Combine the available signals and analyze patterns that may indicate early stress.

### 03 — Assess

Translate the analysis into an understandable stress or risk level.

### 04 — Act

Provide a recommended next step to support faster and more informed decision-making.

## 🧠 Key Features

### 🔬 Multi-Signal Monitoring

FoodGuard considers multiple data sources instead of relying on one indicator.

### ⚠️ Early Stress Detection

The concept focuses on identifying potential stress before it becomes clearly visible.

### 📊 Explainable Risk Levels

The system is designed to communicate results through understandable risk levels rather than presenting raw data alone.

### 💡 Actionable Recommendations

FoodGuard aims to provide a recommended next action instead of simply displaying a warning.

### 🌴 Date Palm Focused

The prototype is specifically designed around monitoring and understanding stress in date palms.

### 🌍 Environmental Context

Environmental and irrigation conditions are considered alongside plant signals to provide a broader picture of palm health.

## 🌍 Why FoodGuard?

FoodGuard is not only about collecting agricultural data.

The goal is to transform data into information that can support better decisions.

The project focuses on three main questions:

> Is the palm showing signs of stress?

> How serious is the potential risk?

> What should be checked or done next?

By bringing these elements together, FoodGuard explores how AI and sensing technologies can support more proactive date-palm monitoring.
