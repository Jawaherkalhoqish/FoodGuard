HOW FOODGUARD WORKS

Multiple signals. One clearer picture of palm health.

Instead of relying only on visible symptoms, FoodGuard brings together the signals around the palm and uses multimodal analysis to support earlier decisions.

01

Sense

Collect plant electrophysiological signals together with soil, weather, temperature, and irrigation data.

02

Understand

Fuse multimodal data, assess risk, and classify potential water-stress conditions.

03

Act Early

Generate an explainable early warning and guide the grower toward a simple recommended action.

THE FOODGUARD PIPELINE

From the palm's signals to an actionable warning.

FoodGuard follows a five-layer architecture designed to move from raw measurements to meaningful information for the grower.

01 Data Sources

Plant, soil, weather & irrigation

02 Preprocessing

Clean, synchronize & normalize

03 AI Analysis

Fusion, detection & risk assessment

04 Cloud

Data storage, models & results

05 Early Warning

Risk, alert & recommended action

EXPLAINABLE ALERTS

Don't just say there is a risk. Explain why.

Early Warning MODERATE RISK
! Potential water-stress signal

The system detected a change across multiple inputs. The combination of soil moisture, temperature, and plant signals suggests increased attention may be needed.

RECOMMENDED ACTION
Inspect irrigation coverage and re-check soil moisture.

Simple actions help growers respond without unnecessary complexity.

Transparency is part of the FoodGuard design. The research findings showed that growers place higher trust in AI alerts when the system explains its reasoning.

✓
Explain the signal

Show which inputs contributed to the warning.

✓
Keep the action simple

Translate complex analysis into an understandable next step.

✓
Support, not replace, the grower

FoodGuard is designed as decision support, keeping human judgment in the loop.
