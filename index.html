THE IDEA

From hidden plant signals to an early warning.

01

Collect

FoodGuard brings together electrophysiological signals, soil conditions, weather, temperature, irrigation, and other relevant data.

02

Analyze

The framework applies multimodal data fusion to identify patterns and assess the potential risk of water stress.

03

Warn & Explain

FoodGuard provides an early warning with an explanation and a recommended action, helping the grower understand what may be happening.

Why multimodal data?

The research proposes comparing environmental and irrigation data, electrophysiological signals alone, and all sources combined — to test whether the plant's own electrical signals add predictive value.

FoodGuard AI is currently a conceptual framework and interface prototype using simulated data. Predictive accuracy and the added value of electrophysiological signals have not yet been validated with real-world data.
